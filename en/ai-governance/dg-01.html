[Data Governance #1] How Can GenAI Strengthen Data Governance in Financial Services?

Jul 25, 20243 min read

Financial institutions have long accepted that data governance matters, yet it rarely comes first when resources are allocated. Over the past few years, the rise of data science, AI and now generative AI (GenAI) has changed that: each step forward in the technology has forced data governance further into the spotlight. This series looks at data governance from several angles.

This first article asks how GenAI can strengthen data governance in financial services.

—

Across our data governance projects, the most common problems among the eight pillars of data governance are data quality, data lineage and the data dictionary, three issues that are closely linked to one another. GenAI can do more than lift the quality and consistency of data: through automated quality checks and cleansing, it helps keep data accurate and reliable. It also brings more transparency and accountability to the data management framework, so institutions can trace and manage their data sources more effectively, which supports sharper decisions and business growth.

Here are three basic ways GenAI can strengthen data governance:

  1. Creating and enriching metadata

In our experience, missing or imprecise metadata is the most common root cause of poor data quality. The biggest obstacle is the filling-in itself: it is tedious, labour-intensive and done without a consistent standard. This is exactly where GenAI can help.

For example, when banks in Taiwan build a system, they produce a business analysis document (BA document) and a system analysis document (SA document). Between them, these two documents usually hold much of the information that metadata needs. Having GenAI read them and generate metadata automatically already makes data easier to understand and reduces the risk of it being misused.

For legacy data, GenAI can also read the large body of existing documentation to fill in metadata that was never captured when older systems were built.

  1. Monitoring, reconciling and cleansing data quality

Catching anomalies, errors and inconsistencies while systems are running is a practical way to strengthen data governance day to day. GenAI can scan large datasets to identify missing values, format errors and outliers, then raise timely alerts so they can be dealt with.

We also often find that fields with the same name in different tables do not agree with each other. There can be many reasons for this; given the right prompts, GenAI can help the data governance team work out which one applies.

  1. Building a knowledge graph of data

A knowledge graph can cover a lot of ground, but missing data lineage alone is a common pain point. GenAI can extract the relationships between data elements from documents, files, system configuration files and even source code that may hold lineage information, and use them to lay the foundation of a knowledge graph. This is another way GenAI can strengthen data governance.

These are some of the basic ways GenAI can strengthen data governance. If you would like to go further, please get in touch. We have extensive experience helping financial institutions with data governance in Taiwan, Hong Kong and Singapore.
