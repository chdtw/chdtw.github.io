About me

Trained as an engineer (Ph.D.), focused on data, knowledge and AI governance in financial services.

I'm David Chung, Associate Partner at Synpulse Taiwan. My engineering Ph.D. taught me to take complex systems apart, model them and test them against data. Since 2017 I have applied that approach to consulting for financial holding companies, banks, insurers and securities firms: from data governance, machine learning models and climate risk to, today, generative AI and knowledge vaults.
